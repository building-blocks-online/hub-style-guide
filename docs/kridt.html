Lines, not gaps

Cells touch and are divided by 1px hairlines, so a screen is one sheet rather than a tray of cards. No shadows, no radius between cells, no gradient anywhere.

Colour only where it means something

Status pills and the single action colour. Everything else is spruce on white. The black block in the nav is where you are.

Figures get a gauge

A thin arc against a light track, with the value beneath at medium weight rather than bold. Charts are monochrome: a hatched band for the target, a dashed line for the reference, one black pill for the point that matters.
